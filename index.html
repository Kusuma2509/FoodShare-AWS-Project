How FoodShare works

Three easy steps can help good food reach someone who needs it.

01

Share a food listing

Restaurants, shops, event organizers, and households add details about surplus food, quantity, pickup time, and location.

02

Connect with a recipient

NGOs and community groups browse listings and contact the donor to coordinate collection.

03

Collect and make an impact

Food is collected at the agreed time. Donors and recipients help reduce waste and support the community.

About the project

Share more.
Waste less.

FoodShare is a student AWS project concept designed to make food donation coordination easier through a simple, accessible web platform.

  • ✓Easy food listings
    Record type, quantity, location, and pickup details.
  • ✓Community connection
    Help donors and recipient organizations discover each other.
  • ✓Cloud-ready design
    Can be hosted using Amazon S3 and CloudFront, with backend services added for real data.
